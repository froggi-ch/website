# Google Ads — first campaign (October 2026)

A small, focused search campaign to spend the CHF 400 Google Ads credit on
people who are actively looking for what Froggi does, and to learn which
searches turn into demo requests.

All ad texts below are checked against Google's limits (headline 30,
description 90, sitelink 25 / 35, callout 25 characters) and follow the
froggi-marketing voice: Sie-form, `ss`, no exclamation marks, only live claims.

---

## How Google Ads works, in short

- You pay **per click**, not per view. Someone searches e.g. *wohnungsabnahme
  app*, your ad shows above the results, and you pay only if they click.
- The price of a click is an auction between advertisers on that search.
  For niche B2B software terms in Switzerland expect very roughly
  **CHF 2–6 per click** — a guess, Google shows real figures once it runs.
- **CHF 400 ≈ 70–150 clicks.** That is enough to learn, not enough to spread
  thin. So: one sector, two ad groups, German-speaking Switzerland only.
- A **conversion** is what counts as success — here, a sent demo request
  from the contact form. Without measuring it you only know clicks, not
  customers (see "Conversion tracking" below).

**The credit:** promotional credit usually only unlocks after you have spent a
qualifying amount yourself within a set time (often 60 days). Read the terms in
the offer e-mail before you start, so the credit isn't lost.

## Setting it up, step by step

1. Go to **ads.google.com**, sign in with the Google account the credit was
   sent to, and redeem the promo code under *Billing › Promotions*.
2. When Google offers a "Smart" campaign, choose **Switch to Expert Mode**
   (small link at the bottom). Smart campaigns hide the controls below.
3. *New campaign* → objective **Create a campaign without a goal's guidance**
   → type **Search**.
4. Untick **Google Display Network** and **Search partners**.
5. Locations: **Switzerland**, under *Location options* pick **Presence**.
   Language: **German**.
6. Budget **CHF 10** per day; bidding **Maximise clicks**, tick *Set a maximum
   cost per click bid limit* → **CHF 4**.
7. Ad schedule: Monday–Friday, 07:00–19:00.
8. Create the two ad groups below: paste the keywords (with their `"…"` and
   `[…]` marks), then one **responsive search ad** each with all headlines
   and descriptions.
9. Add the **sitelinks** and **callouts** at campaign level.
10. Add the **negative keywords** at campaign level.
11. Before publishing, check *Recommendations* — decline anything that adds
    broad match, Display, or "auto-apply".

Payment details you enter yourself; Google may review the ads for a day.

## Conversion tracking

To count demo requests, Google needs a small tag on the site that fires when
the contact form is sent successfully.

1. In Google Ads: *Goals › Conversions › New conversion action › Website* →
   enter `froggi.ch` → **Add a conversion action manually**: name
   *Demo-Anfrage*, category *Submit lead form*, value *don't use*, count *One*.
2. Choose **Use Google tag** → copy the **Conversion ID** (`AW-…`) and the
   **conversion label**.
3. Send those two values back — they go into the contact form's success
   handler, and the privacy page gets a paragraph on Google Ads conversion
   tracking.

## After launch

- **Week 1:** check *Insights › Search terms* every two days; add anything
  irrelevant to the negatives.
- **Week 2:** pause keywords with many clicks and no conversions; raise the
  CPC limit to CHF 5 if impressions are low and the budget is not spent.
- **~15 demo requests:** switch bidding to *Maximise conversions*.
- Judge by **cost per demo request**, not by clicks.

## Check before publishing

- The ads don't name the App Store or Google Play — the iOS app is in
  TestFlight. Keep it that way until both stores are live.
- "Ab CHF 39" is the launch pricing on the website; change the ads if it changes.
