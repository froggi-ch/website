# The four audiences

Froggi's website names four sectors. Each has its own words, objects, pains and buyer. Write in their vocabulary — that is most of what makes copy feel made for them. The buyer (who signs) and the user (who records) differ; a piece usually speaks to the buyer about the user's day.

## Pflege & Betreuung (care homes, home care / Spitex, assisted living)

- **Objects:** Bewohnerinnen und Bewohner, Klientinnen und Klienten, Zimmer, Stationen, Wohngruppen.
- **Actions:** Körperpflege, Duschen, Mobilisation, Hilfsmittel, Wundkontrolle, Sturz, Beobachtung, Übergabe.
- **Users:** Pflegefachpersonen, FaGe, Betreuende — often on shared tablets, in shifts.
- **Buyers:** Heimleitung, Pflegedienstleitung, Leitung Spitex-Organisation.
- **Pains:** documentation eats care time; paper and generic forms don't fit their routines; proving what was done to supervisors, insurers and families; handovers between shifts; staff with different languages.
- **What lands:** own vocabulary instead of a fixed form; fast three-step recording; a resident's whole history in one place; revisions and audit trail ("bleibt belegbar"); offline in rooms with bad Wi-Fi; multilingual forms.
- **Careful:** Froggi is **not** a care-planning (Pflegeplanung), assessment (RAI/BESA) or billing system. The care template is **planned**, not available. Sensitive data: emphasise permissions and audit trail, never claim certifications or Swiss hosting.

## Liegenschaftsverwaltung (property management)

- **Objects:** Liegenschaft, Mieteinheit / Wohnung, Allgemeinbereich (Treppenhaus, Waschküche), Technische Anlage (Heizung, Lift).
- **Actions:** Wohnungsübergabe, Wohnungsabnahme, Mieterwechsel, Leerwohnungskontrolle, Rundgang, Mängelmeldung, Brandschutzkontrolle.
- **Users:** Bewirtschafterinnen und Bewirtschafter, Hauswarte on site.
- **Buyers:** Geschäftsführung, Leitung Bewirtschaftung.
- **Pains:** handover disputes without photos; information scattered across e-mail, paper and the ERP; nobody sees when the last round in a building was; recurring checks forgotten.
- **What lands:** the ready-made **Immobilienbewirtschaftung** template (live, CH, 227 fields) — "am ersten Tag startklar"; photos and location in the report; a building's history at a glance; reminders for recurring rounds; works beside the existing ERP (export, API).

## Hauswartung & Facility (caretaking, facility services)

- **Objects:** buildings, Technische Anlagen, rooms, outdoor areas.
- **Actions:** Kontrollgang, Zählerstand, Heizungskontrolle, Liftkontrolle, Reparatur, Aussenanlage, Winterdienst.
- **Users:** Hauswarte, technicians — on the phone, in cellars and plant rooms.
- **Buyers:** owners of caretaking firms, facility managers.
- **Pains:** no signal in the basement; proving work to the client/owner; recurring checks; many buildings, few people.
- **What lands:** offline recording ("Funktioniert auch im Keller"); interval reminders since the last check; alerts on thresholds (e.g. a meter reading); history per installation as proof to the client.

## Reinigung & Unterhalt (cleaning and maintenance)

- **Objects:** sites, buildings, floors, rooms, areas.
- **Actions:** Unterhaltsreinigung, Grundreinigung, Kontrolle, Reklamation, Materialbestand.
- **Users:** cleaning staff and supervisors, often multilingual.
- **Buyers:** owners and operations managers of cleaning companies.
- **Pains:** proving to the client that cleaning was done; complaints without evidence; mixed-language teams; supervisors checking many sites.
- **What lands:** photo and time-stamped proof; quality checks as their own action; forms in the team's languages; one history per site to show the client.

## General pieces

When a piece speaks to all four, lead with what they share — work done on-site, on objects, that has to be documented and provable — and name the sectors only as examples ("ob Pflege, Bewirtschaftung oder Hauswartung").
